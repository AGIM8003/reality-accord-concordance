## One-paragraph summary

Multi-robot systems must coordinate effects in shared space without exchanging full private world models, while preserving human intervention optionality. We propose REALITY ACCORD: privacy-minimized counterexample exchange, consequence tubes, and Human Optionality Reserve gates that ACCEPT, REJECT, or QUARANTINE proposed effects.
